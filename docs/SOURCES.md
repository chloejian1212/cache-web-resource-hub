## Distinguishing three types of content

| Type | Examples | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published interface | Topic destinations, introductory sections, resource groups, and the tested Education & Training expansion. | Described as observed delivery, with public links and screenshots. |
| Project research and planning | Interview themes, earlier taxonomy, stakeholder feedback, and sustainability proposal. | Used to explain context and design rationale; not automatically treated as deployed features. |
| Recommendations in this repository | Resource metadata fields, maintenance workflow, and follow-up evaluation. | Labeled as proposed or recommended work. |

The earlier taxonomy and the published navigation are deliberately documented separately. There is no assumption that every inventory category became a page or that every published page resulted from a simple rename.

## Measurement and history limits

No final SUS score, verified sample total, task-time reduction, conversion increase, or traffic uplift is reported. Planned study targets and success criteria are not treated as measured results.

The website change tracker and resource tracking sheet were provided as Drive shortcuts, but their contents could not be read. The live website now confirms visible delivery independently of those trackers; a complete change history and final validated resource count remain unavailable.

The preliminary analytics document contains image-based charts whose numerical values were not extracted or reconstructed. Current page availability cannot supply the missing before-and-after data.

The review did not test every external destination, conduct a full accessibility audit, or verify all mobile and browser combinations. The absence of a carousel in the inspected resource-page presentation is not used to reconstruct its exact removal history.

## Material handling and attribution

The public package contains paraphrased research findings and interface screenshots. It excludes raw recordings, participant identities, contact records, internal document links, and raw analytics exports. Draft consent material and claims of research ethics approval are not reproduced.

This is a UX and information-design case study. It does not independently validate the medical or scientific content of external resources, and it does not grant new rights to CACHE’s website or third-party materials. See [Credits](../CREDITS.md).
